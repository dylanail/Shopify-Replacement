# Product research

"You can make any product work"; the question is how hard, and whether a way
to stand out exists before the first dollar is spent.

## Hard criteria

| Criterion | Rule |
|---|---|
| AOV | Over $60 after bundles; US CPMs are fixed and a low AOV cannot absorb a $20–30 cost per purchase |
| Markup | At least 3x landed cost (COGS + shipping); 5x ideal |
| Unit price filter | Average unit price over $15 when scanning TikTok Shop data |
| High ticket | Avoid for beginners; a $170 product at 2x target ROAS has an $85 breakeven CPA and days with zero purchases |
| Trend | Google Trends, US, 5 years: flat or up passes; declining fails; spike-and-crash fails |
| Weight | Avoid heavy items; prefer supplements, skincare, small goods |
| Seasonality | Fine to get going, not for a brand |
| Efficacy | Only sell what works; check with a model and with reviews; no gimmicks |
| Tech and battery | Extra caution; test the physical unit first |
| Legal | Trademark search on the name; never a patented product; comparison ads show the competitor without its brand name |

## Difficulty factors

1. Magnitude of desire (strongest available wins)
2. Market awareness (solution- or product-aware for beginners)
3. Market sophistication (3–5 needs a reset; see `sophistication.md`)

## The walk-through

1. Scan the market data (Kalodata or equivalent): last 7 days, unit price over $15.
2. Disqualify instantly: patented, big-brand, print-on-demand, heavy, too cheap.
3. Trademark search the product name.
4. Brand-size check: a product doing millions on a marketplace with a tiny social following is being carried by the marketplace, not the brand — an opening.
5. Competitor ads in the ad library on the generic keyword: which angles run, whether anyone runs product-aware comparison.
6. Traffic and revenue estimate: monthly visits × 1% (0.1% conservative) × base price.
7. Sentiment: Reddit, Amazon 1–3 star reviews, TikTok comments → pros, cons, the exact complaints. These become angles or reasons to skip.
8. Trend check on the niche keyword and two variants.
9. Price expectation: Amazon's band and a model's estimate; the source cost must allow 3x inside that band.
10. Source cost: reverse image search, then an agent quote; confirm the supplier replicates the mechanism, not just the packaging.
11. AOV engineering: bundles, longer supply, add-ons, gifts to clear $60.
12. Decide the stand-out: list candidate underserved avatars and mechanisms. None → do not run it.
13. Traction proof: the top creatives for the product show what already works; they validate, they do not scale.

## Testing discipline

- Cap the search-and-build at eight hours; launch anyway.
- Assume a 1% hit rate; plan for a hundred intentional tests; review every failure.
- Expect two to three weeks unprofitable before a winning ad.
- Stick to one or two avatars over years; learnings compound.
