# Facebook asset structure and warm-up

Real profiles only; never bought assets. Three real profiles, each admin on
every Business Manager.

| BM | Holds | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pixel BM | Only pixels, shared out | A ban elsewhere keeps the data |
| Ad BM | Nothing owned; pixels and ad accounts shared in; all campaigns run here | Disposable |
| Holding BM (no company) | Ad accounts, shared into the Ad BM | Survives an Ad BM ban |

With an incorporated company: verify the BM and drop to two (Pixel BM and a
verified Ad-account BM). The page is created at the profile level, never
inside a BM, with the other two profiles as admins.

## Warm-up

- Profiles: 5–10 minutes a day of real activity for a week.
- Page: complete every field; one post immediately; two posts a day for three days before ads.
- Ad account: timezone of the market sold to; currency of the bank; card added at BM level with matching addresses.
- Warm-up campaign: page-likes, $5/day, worldwide, duplicated to ~50 approved ads, three days. Only then the sales campaign.
